# Self-critique

Use this checklist for long or high-stakes work, or when the user requests deeper critique.

- Which conclusions remain uncertain, and why?
- Which assumptions were not verified?
- What required work, evidence, edge case, or affected surface may have been missed?
- What is the most plausible later failure or fragile dependency?
- Did later evidence weaken an earlier recommendation?
- Were any constraints, trade-offs, or material dissent compressed away?
- Would independent review or Level 2 DAP materially improve confidence?

Do not invent extra scope, tools, documents, or work to make the result appear more complete.
